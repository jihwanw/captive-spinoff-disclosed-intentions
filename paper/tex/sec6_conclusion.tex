\section{Limitations and conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The evidence is associational throughout, and we have been careful to say so where the temptation to say more is strongest. The controlling block is measured before the announcement and cannot be its consequence. But controllers with small stakes run different firms and groups from controllers with large ones, and nothing in our design isolates the stake from what travels with it. The disposal signal predicts the disposal and the return, and the early disposal absorbs the signal's association with the return. This is the pattern a causal chain from intention to disposal to loss would produce. It is also the pattern that would be produced if filings that read as disposals are written by parents whose businesses were already in decline for reasons the filing does not state and the controls do not capture. The controls in Table~\ref{tab:controls} make the second reading harder to sustain, not impossible.

The return horizon is one year, chosen because \citeA{kang2025} use it and because it is where the early disposals fall. Disposals two or more years out are not associated with a loss within the year, and we do not know whether their parents lose when the disposal comes. A longer window would answer this at the cost of more noise and more overlap with other events. The benchmark is the parent's market index. A size- and book-to-market-matched benchmark would be the usual alternative; the calendar-time portfolio with five-factor adjustment in Tables~\ref{tab:returns_by_score} and~\ref{tab:returns_by_outcome} is our substitute for it. Its point estimates agree with the event-time results and its standard errors do not reach conventional significance, which we report rather than resolve.

The reform results compare filings before and after October 2022 with no untreated group, because the rules applied to every captive spin-off. The post-reform sample is \NPostFilings{} filings, and the outcomes of the later ones are not yet observable. We claim only what that design can show: the filings and their reception changed, and the disposal signal and the early-disposal rate did not within the horizon we observe. The appraisal right has been in force since late December 2022 and the listing review since September 2022, and neither has yet been tested against a contested listing in our data.

The disposal signal is produced by two large language models, and a measure produced by a model has limits of its own. We have addressed the one that matters most, that the model recalls rather than reads, by anonymizing the filings and finding the signal intact, and we have shown that the measure is stable across repeated runs and across the two models. We have not shown what in the filing the models respond to beyond the facts they extract and the sentences they quote. A reader who wants the signal as an explanatory variable rather than a forecast should treat it as the summary of a document, not as a list of its features. The outcome labels come from the parent's reports read in part by a model as well. \NSensitive{} of the 2017--2026 labels depend on a definitional choice or a source conflict, and the Supporting Information shows that reversing all of them does not change the results. For 2001--2016 the prices come from a commercial database whose adjustment factors occasionally fail, and we have excluded the \GlitchExcluded{} events in which a failure is visible and shown the results with them included.

What the evidence supports is this. Korean controlling owners who later disposed of a carved-out subsidiary wrote division filings that differed, in ways a careful reader could detect, from those written by controllers who kept it, and the difference was larger where the controller's stake was smaller. Minority investors received the filings that signalled a disposal more favourably at the announcement and lost more over the following year, with the loss concentrated where the disposal came quickly and where the controlling block was small. A reform that mandated disclosure of purpose and protection changed what the filings said and how they were received before it changed, if it changes, what the filings were about.
